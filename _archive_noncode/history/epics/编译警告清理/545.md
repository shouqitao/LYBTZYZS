### 持续监控机制

#### 1. CI/CD集成
```yaml
# 警告检查流程
build:
  script:
    - dotnet build --configuration Release --verbosity normal | tee build-log.txt
    - warning_count=$(grep -c "warning" build-log.txt || echo "0")
    - echo "Warning count: $warning_count"
    - if [ "$warning_count" -gt 100 ]; then exit 1; fi
```
